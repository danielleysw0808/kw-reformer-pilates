Your instructor

Hi, I'm Kate.

For me, reformer Pilates is never one-size-fits-all. In my private Battersea studio I work with clients one-to-one, building each session around what your body needs that day — whether you're rebuilding strength, easing back pain, or moving safely through pregnancy.

I'm certified in prenatal and postnatal reformer Pilates, and I love guiding clients through every chapter — before, during, and after pregnancy. Expect clear cueing, genuine attention, and a calm space where you can simply focus on you.

The KW approach

What working together looks like

No crowded classes, no guesswork — just a considered, progressive practice that meets you where you are.

01

Every journey starts with a chat about your history, goals, and how your body feels right now — so nothing is generic.
